SUPERVISOR OR TEAM LEAD

Should You Apply?

Yes. Both are a yes.

SUPERVISOR POSITION

Strength - You Have What It Takes

Strength card for Supervisor. Absolutely apply. You have way more capacity for this than you think.

But it's going to be more isolating and more chaotic. More responsibility, more stress. You won't always get to be the good guy.

TEAM LEAD POSITION

Six of Pentacles - Balance

Six of Pentacles for Team Lead. Still a yes, different energy. Balance, fairness, support.

Easier path. Less isolation, less chaos. You lead without carrying everything. Softer step up.

HEADS UP

Don't Expect a Ton of Feedback

If you apply, you might not hear much feedback during the process. That doesn't mean it's not happening. Don't let that freak you out.

THE SHADOW SIDE

The Devil + Page of Cups

The Devil came out as final advice. It's about the temptation to be the good guy all the time. You won't always get to be nice. Everything's got its good and bad parts.

You don't have to be a monster. Just be fair.

BOTTOM LINE

What Should You Do?

Both are a yes. Supervisor: more isolation, more chaos, big growth. Team Lead: easier, balanced, still leadership.

It depends what you want. The challenge or the softer step?

Don't expect constant updates. You'll have to make unpopular calls sometimes. You can handle it.
