04 — Defined

Key Terms

Pert
adjective
Old French · apert — open, bold
Lively, self-assured, and a little cheeky — in the most attractive way. The kind of woman who says exactly what she means and somehow still leaves you wanting more.
Confident. A little cheeky. Unapologetically itself.
Rue
verb / noun
Old English · hrēowan — to grieve, feel regret
To bitterly regret a past action or decision; to feel sorrow or remorse. Also: an evergreen shrub historically associated with repentance and grace.
Regret that hasn't finished speaking yet.

05 — Key Themes

Reading Notes

We got confirmation on his reason for disconnecting and his reason for not reaching out, which you took with stride (something he would never expect). It's time to decide how firm you stand on not being the one to reach out. Whether this connection might be worth taking that step for him since he now sees you as an unattainable goal that he fumbled the ball on the way to.
