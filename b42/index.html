03 — Reading Notes

What Came Through

The Problem: Something to Get Off Your Chest
There's something heavy you're carrying that involves a friendship.

```

What You Want: To Communicate
All the cards about what you want are talking about communication. Using words. Saying what you need to say. You want to get this off your chest and you hope he/she/they receives it well.

What You Need to Do: Try One More Time
It switches up here. There might be an issue between you and your friend where you've both had a situation with the same person. Or whatever you're considering doing could hurt them.

Consequences: Getting Even, Same Cycle Repeating
If you go through with this, it could create a pattern where either they do the same thing back to you, creating a cycle.

Alternative Solution: Time Alone, Don't Come Clean
Your alternative is to spend time by yourself. Not being with that person. The cards show "clear the air" in reverse, which means your alternative solution is to NOT come clean about whatever this is. To hold back.

Consequences of Keeping Quiet: Second guessing
You'll end up saying what you need to say anyway. Trying to hold it back would just be a waste of time.

Oracle Advice: Stairs, Joy of Song, It's OK to Say No
Pay attention to whatever progresses you upward. Pay attention to music right now, specific lyrics. And it's okay to say no if something doesn't feel right.
```
