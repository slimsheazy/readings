About This Reading

This reading covers the next seven days for Jade (Aries) and Carl (Taurus). The theme of your week centers around celebration and going out together. There may be a recent achievement to celebrate, or you might reconnect with someone from your past.

Thursday - Passionate Day

Thursday brings passion and playfulness between you two. There's a high likelihood of intimacy. This is a day to look forward to—the energy is warm, romantic, and deeply connected.

Saturday - Communication Lacking

Sunday - Wishy Washy Energy

Carl in particular may be moody or hard to read on Sunday. He could go back and forth emotionally, leaving you trying to untangle his vibe. Patience and understanding will help navigate this day.
